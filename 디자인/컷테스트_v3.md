```
Create one image: a 2x3 grid of six panels, numbered 1 to 6 in the top-left corner. Studio product photography for a minimal Korean skincare brand.

Every panel shows the same two products, both always visible:
- a 100 ml opaque matte two-tone squeeze tube (upper half warm ivory #F3ECE2, lower half deep burgundy wine #5A1A2A) standing on a flat matte ivory cap
- a 300 ml toner: clear plastic bottle with a thin burgundy wine (#5A1A2A) band around the very bottom and a matte burgundy wine (#5A1A2A) cap; two-layer toner inside, lower 75% clear water and upper 25% pale straw-colored camellia seed oil
Each product has the single word "ONGYEOL" once, in thin sans-serif. No other text on the products.

Every panel must have a different background, a different camera angle and a different product position. No two panels alike.
1. stepped set: an ivory tone-on-tone room; two stacked blocks of different heights, each block is burgundy on the bottom and ivory on top like the tube; tube on the lower block, toner on the higher block; camera slightly below eye level
2. early morning: plain ivory plaster wall with hard sunlight through a window grid falling diagonally across both products, long crisp shadows; camera slightly above, products center-left
3. split backdrop made of two real paper sheets, warm ivory above and deep burgundy below, the products standing on the line where the two colors meet; straight-on eye level, centered
4. top-down flat lay on ivory linen: tube lying diagonally, toner lying parallel, between them two cream swatches side by side, one a moist dollop and one spread into a thin satin film
5. low close-up: in the sharp foreground a split dark camellia seed and one drop of clear golden oil on dark wet stone; both products behind, softly out of focus and partly cropped by the frame edge
6. night bedside: a small ivory linen-covered side table, warm lamp glow from the left, deep shadows; products on a small ceramic tray in the left third; eye level

Realism: matte surfaces, real contact shadows under every object, one consistent light direction per panel, natural colors. Camellia seeds are dark brown, irregular and slightly angular. No people, no flowers, no pink, no bright red, no gold or metallic objects, no water splashes.
```
